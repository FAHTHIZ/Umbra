# Code of conduct

Umbra follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and remember that many people who use privacy tools do so for their safety. Harassment, doxxing, or pressure on anyone to reveal identifying information is never acceptable.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/FAHTHIZ/Umbra/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly.
