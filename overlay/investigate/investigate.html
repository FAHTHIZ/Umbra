  • Separate profile (profile.umbra-investigate): nothing shared with your normal browsing
  • Onion-only: clearnet sites, redirects to them and clearnet subresources are blocked before they connect
  • Uses your current Tor connection. If Tor stops, Investigate loads nothing
  • Security level Safest by default; no passwords or form data are saved
  • Inspection is read-only: Umbra never fills in, submits, solves or bypasses anything
