What this does not do

Tor hides your IP address from websites and hides the sites you visit from your network. It doesn't protect you if you sign in to accounts, open downloaded documents while online, or reveal identifying details. Umbra keeps the same fingerprint as every other Tor Browser user, so avoid changing protections you don't understand.
