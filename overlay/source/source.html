Umbra Source

Contact news organisations securely through SecureDrop, from an isolated Umbra window.

Before you share anything

  • High-risk? Use Tails on a device that isn't your work computer, on a network that isn't your workplace or home. Umbra on your everyday computer is weaker.
  • Don't use a device or network your employer controls. Don't search for the outlet from your normal browser.
  • Remove metadata from documents. Photos and PDFs can reveal your device, your location and you.
  • SecureDrop gives you a codename. Write it down on paper. It's the only way to read replies.

Umbra doesn't submit anything itself. It opens the organisation's own SecureDrop onion page.

How Source mode is isolated

  • A separate Umbra profile: no shared history, cookies, storage, permissions or downloads with your normal browsing
  • Security level Safest: JavaScript is off (SecureDrop works without it)
  • Uses your current Tor connection with its own circuits. If Tor stops, Source mode loads nothing.
  • No Umbra background requests (release check and Access Engine are off)
  • Opens only .onion addresses from the directory below
